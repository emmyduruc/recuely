Capture lab (SPEC.md Task 9)

Throwaway test page. Nothing leaves your network: results go only to the Mac running this page, and only when you tap Send results. Run the sections in order; each one takes under a minute. The mic and camera turn on only when you tap a button, and the badge at the top right shows when they're live.

A. Capabilities

Runs automatically.

B. Permission & track lifecycle

Tap Allow and accept the prompt. Check that the badge turns red, then tap Stop and check that it turns off.


    


    

      
C. Echo tail (speaker)

      
Use the built-in speaker at your normal volume, with no headphones. Stay quiet: the page plays a short phrase 6 times (3 with echo cancellation on, 3 with it off) and measures what the mic still hears after it ends.

      
      

    


    

      
D. Take start clipping

      
10 short takes. When the dot turns green, say "Paper" once, clearly. The recorder starts when your voice is detected; the page measures how much of the "P" it lost.

        
      

    


    

      
E. Recording formats & sizes

      
Records 3 s of video in each format this browser supports. Download a file or two and open them in your system player (Photos, Files or QuickTime) to check they play.

      
      

      

    


    

      
F. Joining two takes

      
Records two 2 s takes (wave at the camera), then tries two ways to join them. Download both results and check they play for about 4 s.

      
      

      

    


    

      
G. Audio output

      
Lists outputs and plays the phrase on one. If you have Bluetooth earbuds, connect them first. Then say where you heard it.

        
      
      
      

    


    

      
H. Background tab & lock screen

      
Tap Start. Then switch to another app or tab for about 5 s, come back, lock the screen for about 5 s, unlock, and tap Finish.
